🔒 Privacy · ↩ Clock

Short version: your receipts stay on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

No accounts

There is nothing to sign up for. The app doesn't know who you are.

No tracking or analytics

No analytics, ads, trackers or cookies. The app doesn't contact any other company's servers. It only loads its own files from the site that hosts it, GitHub Pages (ouflyer71.github.io). Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.

Receipts stay on your device

Receipt photos, stores, items, prices, dates and notes are stored only in this browser on this device. Reading the receipt (text recognition) runs on your phone using a copy of the Tesseract engine that is part of the app itself. Nothing is uploaded.

Voice

The 🎤 button uses your phone's built-in speech recognition. The app only receives the finished text. Depending on your phone's settings, your phone maker (e.g. Apple) may process the audio.

Backups

Backups are a single file you save wherever you choose. If you lose your phone without a backup, your receipts can't be recovered, because no copy exists anywhere else.
